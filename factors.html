A factor divides exactly into a number, with nothing left over.

Work in pairs: start with 1 × the number, then try 2, 3, 4 and so on. Each factor you find brings its partner with it. When the pairs meet in the middle, you have them all.

Times tables help: 6 × 4 = 24, so 6 and 4 are both factors of 24.
